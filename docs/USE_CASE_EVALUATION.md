## Decision

**Observation:** all high-scoring ideas rely on web interfaces plus a need to turn fragments into auditable, operational work—not just extract a single page. Property distress gives the clearest 90-second visual narrative: a new event, independent corroboration, a property event graph, and proof of what the agent did.

**Inference:** AcreBrief is the strongest rapid vertical slice because a Lee County source graph can be shown without contact data or outreach tooling, while expanding naturally into title, tax, code, and acquisition workflows.

**Risk:** title-curative and litigation products may ultimately have higher willingness to pay, but require more legal-risk controls and longer integrations. We retain the parcel/evidence graph as an option layer, not a disposable scraper.
