# AcreBrief product definition

## Promise

**Know which newly distressed Southwest Florida properties deserve attention before an acquisition team spends hours across county websites.**

## Personas and jobs

| Persona | Job | Current friction | Desired proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acquisition analyst | triage today’s changes | repetitive county tabs, uncertain matches, no audit trail | ranked brief with evidence and unknowns |
| Small/midsize investor | decide where to investigate | paid lists lack context | one defensible property story, not a contact list |
| Broker/investor team | qualify a potential opportunity | teams repeat research inconsistently | shared timeline and source provenance |
| Title/property-data organization | resolve public-record fragments | source normalization and QA burden | stable IDs, raw-to-normalized lineage |

## Minimum commercial product

Lee County first: new filing/record signal → conservative parcel resolution → property and related record checks → tax signal when authorized → timeline → explainable score → evidence brief. Charlotte and Collier are source-registry-backed expansion candidates, not fake enabled adapters.

## Pricing hypothesis (not a published offer)

- Founding concierge pilot: $750–$1,500/month for AcreBrief to work one real buy box and deliver source-backed investigations; a $500 two-week paid beta is the lowest-friction willingness-to-pay test.
- Pilot cohort: 3–10 qualified local teams; interview-led and explicitly opt-in.
- Starter: $299–499/month for a small team, daily event brief and limited investigations.
- Team: $1,000–2,000/month for shared watchlists, historical exports, and higher investigation allocation.
- Data/enterprise: custom pricing only after licensing, audit controls, and support needs are established.

These are hypotheses to test against time saved and quality of investigated opportunities. There are currently no claimed paying users, testimonials, or results.

## Metrics

Track investigations started/completed/partial; events detected/deduped; resolved versus review-required properties; brief views; evidence clicks; replay views; watchlist additions; exports; pilot requests; feedback; source error rate; browser/sandbox time; cache hit ratio; and cost per completed investigation. Never infer a metric from a demo view.

## Pilot sign-up and feedback

Pilot intake should request work email, company, market, team size, current research workflow, and consent to follow up. It must explain that no contact data is sold or outreach automatically initiated. A short post-brief prompt asks: “Did this change deserve the time it saved? What source was missing?”
