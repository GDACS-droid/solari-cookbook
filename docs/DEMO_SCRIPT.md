# 80-second demo script

**0–10s — pain.** “Acquisition teams lose hours bouncing among clerk, court, tax, and property-appraiser sites just to decide whether one new filing deserves a call—or another hour.”

**10–20s — evidence.** Open the AcreBrief queue. In the recorded fallback, say: “This is a verified August 31 fixture: two public-record signals, one candidate property, three evidence artifacts, and three important unknowns.” In an authorized live demo only, replace that sentence with the fresh run timestamp and observed counts.

**20–40s — action.** Click **Investigate**. For the verified fallback: “This replays the exact saved evidence and never claims a live Solari session.” For an authorized live run: “AcreBrief checks only the server-approved sources. The checkmarks are source-specific; a gated or failed source stays visible instead of turning green.” Show a replay only if it contains generic privacy-safe portals and has been reviewed.

**40–55s — result.** Open the refreshed result. “Here are the event signals, the candidate property join, and the time-ordered evidence trail. Every supported claim links to its exact artifact and says when it was retrieved.”

**55–70s — why.** Expand score explanation. “The score is explainable: recency and verified distress signals add points; unresolved parcel/encumbrance questions reduce confidence. It never invents a mortgage balance or says a property is a deal.”

**70–80s — differentiation.** Open evidence/replay and pilot CTA. “One agent, fragmented government systems, evidence attached. AcreBrief tells the acquisition team which changes deserve a human next—not who to harass.”

## Recording checklist

- Use verified-sample mode unless a production key, approved source route, and redacted safe property are available.
- Do not show aerial/map imagery, contact information, cookies, profile IDs, source credentials, or unreviewed court/document content.
- Use one continuous screen recording; add only legible captions and no fabricated metrics/testimonials.
