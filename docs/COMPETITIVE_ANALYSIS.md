# Competitive analysis

## What existing products already cover

| Product/category | Existing strength | AcreBrief should not claim to replace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PropStream | broad investor workflows, lead lists, property and marketing tools | broad national data platform or a guaranteed current title/equity source |
| BatchLeads / BatchData | list building, enrichment and skip tracing workflows | licensed contact enrichment, outreach compliance, or contact accuracy |
| PropertyRadar | parcel/property data, maps and investor workflows in supported markets | a full market coverage/map platform |
| DealMachine | investor lead capture and marketing workflow | driving-for-dollars/mobile CRM and outreach workflow |
| Foreclosure.com / PropertyOnion | foreclosure/tax-deed listings and investor research | exhaustive auction inventory or title assurance |
| ATTOM / Regrid | licensed parcel, property, AVM, and enrichment datasets | a licensed national data feed without a vendor contract |
| County clerks/appraisers | primary record, parcel, sale and assessment information | legal record authority, title examination, or correct/current completeness |

## Sharper wedge

The thesis cannot be “we have pre-foreclosure data.” Competitors and county sites already provide versions of that. The defensible workflow is:

1. detect the **change**, not merely a static list;
2. resolve it conservatively to a parcel/event graph;
3. fan out to independent primary sources;
4. preserve the exact evidence, retrieval time, and uncertainty;
5. rank only the handful that merit an acquisition analyst’s next hour; and
6. let a customer watch/replay the collection workflow when safe.

## Thesis under attack

- **“County data is enough.”** It is fragmented, delayed, and source-specific; a property has to be reconciled across instruments, court cases, tax pages, and parcel records. But aggregation does not make a title opinion.
- **“Large providers already do this.”** They may have broader data. AcreBrief must win on transparent evidence-backed investigation, a local workflow, and speed from event to brief—not on pretending to out-license ATTOM.
- **“Automation is unreliable.”** Correct; hence source gates, bulk-first collection, idempotent diffs, evidence, health status, and a human-review queue. Any source with unapproved automation remains off.
- **“This is a people dossier.”** It must remain property-centered, minimize personal data, and exclude contact/outreach automation. Entity records are only a resolution crosswalk.

## Positioning test

If the product only displays a foreclosure list, it has failed. If an analyst can press one button, see the sources checked and exact evidence, understand the unknowns and score in a minute, and decide whether to spend more time, it has earned differentiation.
